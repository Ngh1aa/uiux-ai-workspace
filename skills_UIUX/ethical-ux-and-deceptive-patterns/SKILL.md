# Ethical UX & Deceptive Pattern Prevention

## Workflow
1. Identify moments involving money, consent, privacy, subscription or irreversible commitment.
2. Compare prominence of accept versus decline/cancel choices.
3. Check hidden fees, forced continuity, preselection, fake urgency/scarcity and confirmshaming.
4. Make cancellation/reversal proportionate to signup effort.
5. Surface material terms before commitment.
6. Document business-pressure conflicts as risk, not UX optimization.

## Quality gate
No fake urgency/scarcity, hidden material cost, manipulative consent or intentional obstruction of the user's preferred action.

## Baseline
FTC "Bringing Dark Patterns to Light" consumer-protection guidance.

# Inclusive Design & Cognitive Accessibility

## Workflow
1. Identify exclusion risks across permanent, temporary and situational contexts.
2. Use familiar hierarchy, predictable interaction and clear page purpose.
3. Reduce memory load; keep important instructions/context available.
4. Chunk tasks/content and remove unnecessary distraction.
5. Make primary actions, progress and findability obvious.
6. Support reorientation after interruption.
7. Include relevant users in research/testing.

## Quality gate
- WCAG conformance is treated as a floor, not complete cognitive usability.
- Important tasks do not depend on remembering hidden context.
- Similar tasks behave consistently.
- Motion/distraction is intentionally controlled.

## Baseline
W3C Cognitive Accessibility Guidance and supplemental COGA patterns.

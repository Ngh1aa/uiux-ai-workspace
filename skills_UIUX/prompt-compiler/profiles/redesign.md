# Profile — Redesign

Use when the existing product/repository is authoritative for product truth, data contracts, journeys, or constraints, but UX/visual structure is expected to change materially.

## Preserve

Preserve only verified product truth that should survive:
- data/API contracts;
- required routes/journeys;
- business rules;
- accessibility/legal constraints;
- known working capabilities;
- content that the brief explicitly retains.

Existing DOM/CSS/component structure is not automatically protected.

## Compile

- verified current-state audit;
- evidence-backed gap analysis;
- research/benchmark insights;
- revised IA and critical journeys;
- art direction and anti-template rules;
- design-system/component/state contract;
- migration/implementation boundaries;
- responsive/accessibility behavior;
- rendered QA and regression checks for preserved product behavior.

New design values are `PROPOSED`. Do not call them `VERIFIED` merely because they appear in the compiled spec.

# Research Synthesis & Insight Management

## Workflow
1. Normalize notes into atomic observations.
2. Tag by user, task and context rather than proposed solution.
3. Cluster repeated patterns while preserving contradictory evidence.
4. Write insights as evidence + interpretation + consequence.
5. Distinguish fact, hypothesis, opportunity and decision.
6. Attach confidence and affected journey.
7. Link design decisions back to the evidence they respond to.

## Rules
- Frequency is not the same as importance.
- Do not discard outliers that reveal severe failure or exclusion.
- Avoid persona theater unsupported by evidence.
- Keep raw evidence accessible enough to audit conclusions.

## Output
Evidence repository, affinity clusters, insights, opportunity areas, confidence notes and decision links.

## Quality gate
Every important insight can be traced to evidence and every major design response states what evidence it addresses.

# Education Website Playbook

## Primary journeys

### Parent/prospective family
`Discover → understand philosophy/program → assess fit → compare campus/grade → trust outcomes → admissions → visit/apply`

### Student
`Explore school life/program → clubs/events/facilities → understand expectations → next action`

### Staff/candidate
`Understand culture → roles → requirements → application`

## Core IA

Tùy cấp học nhưng thường gồm:

- About/School story.
- Academic programmes/curriculum.
- Admissions.
- Campuses/facilities.
- Student life/extracurricular.
- Outcomes/results/university pathways.
- News/events/resources.
- Careers.
- Contact/visit.

## Homepage priority

Không biến homepage thành brochure dài. Ưu tiên school promise, age/program navigation, trust proof, campus/experience, outcomes và admissions CTA.

## Admissions UX

Phải làm rõ:

- Entry grades/ages.
- Process steps.
- Required information/documents.
- Fees hoặc đường dẫn rõ nếu được công khai.
- Timeline/intake nếu applicable.
- Visit/tour/enquiry option trước application commitment cao.

## Content proof

Ưu tiên ảnh thật, campus thật, teacher/student experience, accreditation, curriculum facts, outcomes và safeguarding/wellbeing information phù hợp. Tránh stock image không phản ánh môi trường học.

## Mobile

Phụ huynh thường research nhanh trên mobile: menu, campus finder, phone/contact, fees/admissions và form phải rất dễ dùng.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Parent journey tới admissions rõ.
- [ ] Programme/curriculum dễ phân biệt theo age/grade.
- [ ] Campus và school-life có evidence thật.
- [ ] Admissions steps/CTA không mơ hồ.
- [ ] Mobile form/contact được ưu tiên.

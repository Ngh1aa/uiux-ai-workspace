# Content Architecture Reference

## Page job template

```markdown
Page:
Primary audience:
Entry intent:
Question to answer:
Decision to enable:
Primary CTA:
Secondary CTA:
Proof needed:
Objections:
Next page/state:
```

## Common narrative sequence

Không phải template bắt buộc, nhưng hữu ích:
1. Promise/value.
2. Context/problem.
3. Solution/capability.
4. Proof/results.
5. Detail/options.
6. Risk reduction/FAQ.
7. Decision CTA.

Reorder theo journey. Ví dụ returning user có thể cần finder/search trước brand story.

## Microcopy rules

Button: verb + outcome/context.
Form help: explain why/format only when needed.
Error: what happened + what to do.
Empty: why empty + useful next action.
Success: confirm what happened + what happens next.

## Trust

Use evidence nearest relevant claim:
- accreditation/certification near program claim;
- case metric near capability;
- response-time/privacy statement near lead form;
- genuine testimonial near high-friction decision.

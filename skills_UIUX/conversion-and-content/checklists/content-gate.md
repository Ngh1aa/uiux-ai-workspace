# Content & Conversion Gate

- [ ] Primary audience/intent clear.
- [ ] H1/value proposition specific.
- [ ] Above-fold next action clear.
- [ ] Section order follows decision journey.
- [ ] Claims have real proof or are softened appropriately.
- [ ] Primary vs secondary CTA hierarchy clear.
- [ ] No fabricated social proof/urgency.
- [ ] Form/help/error/success copy supports recovery.
- [ ] Mobile scan: headings, short paragraphs, lists/data patterns where useful.
- [ ] SEO intent answered without stuffing.
- [ ] Brand voice consistent.

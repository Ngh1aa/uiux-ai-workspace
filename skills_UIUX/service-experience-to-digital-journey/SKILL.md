# Service Experience → Digital Journey

## Principle
Digitize the logic and meaning of the service experience, not the physical world literally.

`real-world journey → meaningful moments → user questions/emotions → digital representation → next action`

## Workflow
### 1. Map the real experience
Record what happens before, during and after the service:
- trigger/discovery;
- arrival/orientation;
- exploration;
- interaction with people/products/environment;
- evaluation;
- commitment/transaction;
- follow-up/support.

### 2. Identify experience-bearing moments
Find moments that materially influence trust, understanding, emotion or decision. Not every physical step deserves a digital equivalent.

### 3. Identify what cannot be experienced online
Examples: touch, scale, ambience, human interaction, taste, acoustics, physical convenience. Decide how to represent evidence honestly using media, comparison, stories, spatial context, samples, visit booking or human assistance.

### 4. Design digital equivalents
Examples:
- campus visit → guided campus explorer + student/teacher context + visit booking;
- building tour → location → arrival → lobby → amenities → workspace → views → availability → site visit;
- furniture showroom → inspiration → material/color → dimensions → room context → compare/save → buy/visit.

### 5. Preserve a path to the real service
When the physical experience matters, digital should prepare and hand off rather than pretend to replace it completely.

## Required artifact
`docs/service-to-digital-journey.md` with:
- current real-world journey;
- key moments and emotions;
- digital equivalent/representation;
- evidence gaps;
- online/offline handoffs;
- proposed ideal journey.

## Gate
A digital feature must represent a real user need, service moment or decision—not exist merely to feel immersive.

## Anti-patterns
- Literal 3D/skeuomorphic simulation without user value.
- Hiding practical information behind cinematic storytelling.
- Pretending imagery is equivalent to physical proof.
- Ending the web journey at a lead form when the service continues offline.

# Interview Guide

## Study status
`PLANNED_VALIDATION`

## Opening

- Thank participant and explain the study purpose in neutral terms.
- Explain recording/note-taking and consent.
- State that the product/design is being evaluated, not the participant.
- Invite the participant to decline any question or stop at any time.

## Context warm-up

1. Tell me about the last time you **[relevant task]**.
2. What triggered that situation?
3. What were you trying to achieve?
4. Who else was involved?
5. Which tools, people or workarounds did you use?

## Current behavior

- Walk me through what you actually did, step by step.
- Where did you hesitate, switch tools, ask for help, or double-check something?
- What information did you need before you felt comfortable moving on?
- What happens when something goes wrong?
- What do you do today that you wish you did not have to do?

## Closing

- What did I not ask that matters to this workflow?
- Is there anything about this situation that changes under time pressure, higher stakes, mobile use, poor connectivity, accessibility needs, or collaboration?

## Moderator guardrails

- Do not pitch the solution.
- Do not ask “Would you use this?” as primary evidence.
- Do not turn one memorable quote into a finding without supporting context.
- Separate observation from interpretation in notes.
- Record contradictory evidence.

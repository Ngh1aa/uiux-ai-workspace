## Opening

Explain consent/recording, that the interface is being tested rather than the participant, and that the moderator may stay quiet during tasks so natural behavior can be observed.

## Pre-task context

- When would you normally need to do **[task]**?
- What would you expect to know before starting?

## Task template

### Task T-01 — [goal, not UI instruction]

**Scenario:** Give the user a realistic goal without naming the control or route they should use.  
**Decision/hypothesis:** D-__ / H-__  
**Observe:** first action, path, hesitation, errors, recovery, comprehension, confidence, help-seeking, accessibility friction.  
**Do not coach unless:** the session would otherwise become unsafe or completely blocked; record any intervention.

**Post-task probes:**
- What did you expect to happen?
- What, if anything, felt unclear or risky?
- How would you know the task was complete/correct?

## Measures

Use measures as evidence, not vanity scores:

- task outcome: completed / partial / blocked;
- critical error or recovery;
- comprehension of consequential states/content;
- confidence/risk signal where relevant;
- moderator intervention required;
- accessibility/support issue observed.

Do not publish percentages from tiny samples as population estimates.

## Debrief

- Which part required the most thought?
- What would you change first if this were part of your real workflow?
- Was anything missing that you would need before trusting/completing the task?

## Evidence handling

Create/append ledger entries after the session. Keep participant identity separate from research findings where possible. A task result becomes a finding only after synthesis across relevant evidence and decision context.

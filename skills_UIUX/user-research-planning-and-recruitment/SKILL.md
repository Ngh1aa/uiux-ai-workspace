# User Research Planning & Recruitment

## Goal
Turn risky assumptions into answerable research questions and recruit people who can provide relevant evidence.

## Workflow
1. Translate assumptions and product risks into prioritized research questions.
2. Choose method based on the question, not convenience.
3. Define participant characteristics, exclusions and accessibility/support needs.
4. Write recruitment brief and screener without leaking desired answers.
5. Define consent, privacy/data handling, incentives and session logistics.
6. Assign moderator, observer and note-taking roles.
7. Decide how evidence will be synthesized before sessions begin.

## Operational package

When the work needs a runnable study package instead of planning notes alone, use the templates owned by `research-evidence-pipeline`:

- `templates/research-plan.md` — decision-to-question map and logistics;
- `templates/screener.md` — behavior-led participant screening;
- `templates/interview-guide.md` — discovery/context protocol;
- `templates/usability-test-script.md` — task-based solution validation;
- `templates/evidence-ledger.schema.json` — traceable evidence ingestion contract.

Do not create findings or fill the evidence ledger until real source evidence exists.

## Decision rules
- Recruit actual or likely users, not convenient coworkers, unless explicitly doing expert review.
- Include relevant disabled, low-confidence, edge-context or support-needing users when they are part of the audience.
- Collect only participant data needed for the study.
- Separate demographic quotas from behavioral characteristics.

## Output
Research plan, participant profile, screener, consent/data plan, session schedule and synthesis plan. For substantial evidence-led work, prefer the canonical `docs/research/` package defined by `research-evidence-pipeline` so later synthesis and decisions remain traceable.

## Quality gate
- Research questions map to product decisions.
- Participant criteria reflect the real user population.
- No leading recruitment criteria.
- Team knows how findings will be captured and used.
- A completed plan is never reported as evidence that sessions occurred.

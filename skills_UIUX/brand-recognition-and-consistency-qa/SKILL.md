# Brand Recognition & Consistency QA

## Principle
Consistency is not making every section identical. It is preserving a recognizable system while allowing content-appropriate variation.

## Review dimensions
Audit representative pages and states for:
- typography hierarchy/rhythm;
- color roles and contrast;
- spacing/grid behavior;
- recurring brand cues;
- image/video art direction;
- iconography/illustration;
- controls and CTA treatment;
- surface/border/radius/elevation;
- motion behavior;
- voice/microcopy;
- mobile adaptation.

## Recognition heuristic
Test representative cropped sections without relying on logo/header/footer. Ask:
- Are there at least 2–3 recurring cues tied to the project's visual signature?
- Are those cues actually distinctive, or generic category defaults?
- Is recognition carried consistently across desktop/mobile and different templates?

Do not report a numeric recognition score unless real user/market testing exists.

## Drift detection
Flag:
- one-off local colors/type/spacing;
- duplicate components with conflicting expression;
- pages that look like different brands;
- accidental trend injections;
- legacy/new-system mismatch without intentional transition strategy.

## Output
Create a QA table:
`screen → issue → system rule violated → severity → fix → verified state`.

## Gate
P0 journeys and representative templates pass visual-system consistency review at target viewports.

## Anti-patterns
- "Looks consistent to me" without sampling multiple templates.
- Enforcing sameness where task needs differ.
- Fixing brand drift by adding more logos.
- Ignoring mobile because desktop carries stronger branding.

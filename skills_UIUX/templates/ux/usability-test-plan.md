## Session script

1. Welcome, consent, "we're testing the design, not you".
2. Warm-up: how they handle this today.
3. Tasks with think-aloud. Neutral probes: "What are you looking for?", "What did you expect to happen?"
4. Do not help until the failure point is recorded.
5. Debrief: what was hardest, what was missing.

# Trust Gate

Each item is pass or fail.

- [ ] Every trust-critical claim is in the claim-evidence matrix with source, date and owner.
- [ ] Claims without evidence are qualified, rewritten or removed; no unnamed awards or unsourced superlatives remain.
- [ ] Evidence for each decision-point claim is visible or linked inline at that decision point.
- [ ] Legal identity and a human contact route with expected response time are reachable in one step from any page.
- [ ] Every logo has a documented, current relationship, permission and an accurate label.
- [ ] Every testimonial is from a real, consenting person; edits keep meaning; material connections are disclosed next to it.
- [ ] No outcome testimonial implies atypical results are typical.
- [ ] Reviews state their source and filtering; negative reviews are not suppressed.
- [ ] Volatile facts carry a real review date and owner; no auto-refreshing dates.
- [ ] Representative imagery or data that could be mistaken for real is labelled.
- [ ] Links, contact forms and verification links work; no placeholder or typo-level errors on decision pages.
- [ ] Jurisdiction-specific endorsement and review rules have been checked against current official guidance.

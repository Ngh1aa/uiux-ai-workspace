# Trust, Credibility & Transparency

## Workflow
1. List trust-critical claims and user decisions.
2. Map evidence: people, clients, projects, certifications, policies, dates, contacts and third-party proof.
3. Place evidence near the decision it supports.
4. Use authentic imagery/data where available; label representative content.
5. Expose ownership, contact routes and content recency.
6. Remove or soften unsupported superlatives.

## Quality gate
- Major claims have evidence or qualified wording.
- Identity/contact information is easy to find.
- Stale/unknown content is not presented as current fact.
- Logos/testimonials do not imply relationships without support.

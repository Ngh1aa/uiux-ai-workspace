# Nonprofit Website Playbook

## Primary journeys

- Supporter: mission → impact → trust → donate/volunteer.
- Partner: programme fit → evidence → collaboration contact.
- Beneficiary: find help/service → eligibility → access path.

Không trộn các audience này thành một CTA duy nhất.

## Trust architecture

Use impact data with source/context, reports, governance, partners, programme evidence và clear contact information. Financial/transparency documents phải findable nếu relevant.

## Storytelling

Human stories nên tôn trọng dignity/consent, không dùng shock/guilt như default conversion tactic. Balance story với systemic/program evidence.

## Donation UX

- Amount/options understandable.
- Recurring vs one-time distinction clear.
- Explain what happens after donate.
- Fees/data consent transparent.
- Confirmation + receipt/support info.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Mission và actual programmes liên kết rõ.
- [ ] Impact claims có evidence/context.
- [ ] Donate/volunteer/seek-help paths phân biệt.
- [ ] Transparency information findable.
- [ ] Storytelling không dùng dark emotional manipulation.

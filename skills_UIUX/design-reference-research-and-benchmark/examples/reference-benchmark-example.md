# Reference Benchmark Example — International School Homepage

> Example structure only. URLs/scores must be produced from actual research at execution time.

## Project decision

- Domain: international school.
- Audience: parents comparing schools, primarily mobile entry.
- Business goal: communicate stature + programme confidence, then drive admissions inquiry/campus visit.
- Brand: established, premium, academic; avoid childish visual language.
- Constraint: real campus/student photography available; avoid heavy 3D/WebGL.

## Final references

### Reference A — Admissions hierarchy

- What works: parent questions are answered in decision order; CTA is visible without overwhelming content.
- Transferable: sequence `stature → programmes → proof → campus → admissions`.
- Do not copy: brand copy, photography treatment or exact composition.
- Adaptation: use project programme structure and campus proof.
- Caveat: test mobile CTA density.

### Reference B — Programme navigation

- What works: programmes are differentiated by age/stage without card overload.
- Transferable: progressive disclosure and clear next-page labels.
- Adaptation: preserve project sitemap and SEO URLs.

### Reference C — Hero / typography

- What works: large editorial type creates institutional stature and a single dominant focal point.
- Transferable: display scale + restrained supporting copy + full-bleed photography.
- Do not copy: distinctive motion choreography.
- Adaptation: reduce motion, keep LCP image performant and maintain readable Vietnamese/English typography.

## Extracted design DNA

- Layout grammar: editorial full-bleed moments alternating with structured information bands.
- Hierarchy: one dominant message per viewport; headings carry most visual personality.
- Typography: authoritative display + readable sans body; validate Vietnamese diacritics.
- Color: primary brand color used for institutional anchors/CTA, not every surface.
- Imagery: authentic campus/student photography, wide environmental frames + selective close human moments.
- Motion: restrained reveal/orientation; no blanket scroll animation.
- Conversion: admissions CTA after proof and persistent mobile access when justified.

# Human Governance & Playbacks

## Principle

`user outcome → decision owner → evidence → cross-functional review → explicit decision → recorded rationale`

Governance should reduce misalignment and hidden risk, not create meeting theater.

## Human roles

For consequential work identify the human or authorized role for:

- product/business outcome;
- design quality;
- engineering feasibility/system reality;
- user research/evidence;
- privacy/security/compliance when applicable;
- production release.

One person may hold multiple roles in a small project. UNKNOWN ownership must stay explicit.

## Outcome statement

Frame the destination as a user outcome, not a prescribed feature.

Useful structure:

`Who → needs to accomplish what meaningful outcome → under what observable success condition`

This is direction, not implementation.

## Playback/review moments

Use the lightest set appropriate to risk:

1. **Problem playback** — are we solving an evidenced problem for the right user?
2. **Concept/design playback** — do major choices fit the outcome and evidence?
3. **Delivery playback** — walk the end-to-end working experience, not isolated screens.
4. **Release decision** — review unresolved risk, verification, measurement and rollback/learning readiness.

A playback is not approval by applause. Record disagreements and conditions.

## Representative user participation

Recurring representative users/design partners can join reviews to keep the team grounded. They complement formal research; they do not replace it.

## AI boundary

AI agents can:

- assemble evidence;
- surface conflicts;
- propose alternatives/trade-offs;
- draft decision records;
- run technical/visual verification.

AI agents must not silently:

- invent stakeholder agreement;
- approve their own high-consequence recommendation;
- claim legal/compliance sign-off;
- assume production release authority.

## Decision record

For material decisions capture:

`decision → owner → evidence → alternatives → trade-off → dissent/UNKNOWN → approval/condition → revisit trigger`

## Output

Create `docs/decision-governance.md` for substantial production/high-risk work.

## Quality gate

- Decision rights are explicit.
- User outcome remains visible through delivery.
- Major disagreements/UNKNOWNs are not erased.
- Human gates are reserved for materially consequential decisions.
- Release authority is separate from agent confidence.
